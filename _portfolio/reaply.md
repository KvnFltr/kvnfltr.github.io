I co-founded **[Reaply](https://reaply.ai)** to help businesses understand customer feedback and manage responses across multiple locations.

## Product

The deployed product works with **Google reviews**: sentiment analysis, response generation and cross-location benchmarking. Its operational footprint covers **300+ locations**, across businesses of different sizes.

## My contribution

I work across product development, customer discovery and sales: understanding operational needs, defining workflows, shaping the product and bringing it to customers. The experience connects my technical interests to the practical constraints of shipping AI features people use.

## Engineering context

Review analysis and response generation are different tasks: an internal summary and a public-facing reply have different requirements. Working on Reaply has made relevance, tone, configuration and customer control concrete product concerns.

The application is a commercial product with private source code. [Visit Reaply](https://reaply.ai).
